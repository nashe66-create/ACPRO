# ACPRO

ACPRO is the working repository for a UK residential air-conditioning marketplace and workflow platform.

## Product

A transaction marketplace with workflow infrastructure for domestic AC.

Homeowners submit structured AC installation requirements. Qualified installers receive opportunities, submit offers, and compete for work. The selected installer completes a technical survey, confirms the final price, schedules and completes the installation, and receives a review.

## Product evolution

1. Assess — structured property/room information and estimated cooling loads.
2. Recommend — suggested system architecture, with installer control.
3. Select — manufacturer and equipment selection.
4. Validate — technical compatibility and installation constraints.
5. Generate — bill of materials and costing.
6. Procure — merchant workflow and eventually integrations.
7. Transact — payments, finance, contracts and scheduling.
8. Operate — warranties, servicing, repeat work and installer operating tools.

## MVP boundary

### In scope
- Homeowner property and room capture
- Photos and installation characteristics
- Customer requirements
- Estimated cooling loads
- Installer matching
- Installer bidding
- Quote comparison
- Installer selection
- Technical survey
- Final quote
- Installation workflow
- Completion and reviews

### Out of scope
- Automatic manufacturer/equipment selection
- Full compatibility engine
- Automatic pipe/cable sizing or routing
- Full automated BOM
- Merchant API/live stock and pricing
- Integrated finance application
- Customer payment processing
- Escrow

## Working rule

Ideas are captured in the backlog. They do not derail the current deliverable. The next unfinished deliverable on the roadmap determines what we work on next.
